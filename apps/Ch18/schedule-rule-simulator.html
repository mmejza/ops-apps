Scheduling Rule Simulator

Practice FCFS, SPT, and EDD on a five-job single-machine schedule. All jobs arrive at time zero, so completion time equals flow time.

1) Generated Job Data

Each scenario uses jobs A-E with random processing times and due dates.

Job Processing Time (days) Due Date (days)

2) Choose Scheduling Rule

Select a rule, then enter your sequence and calculations.

3) Enter Sequence and Job-by-Job Results

Fill sequence positions 1-5 and compute each row for your chosen schedule. Lateness = Completion - Due Date. Tardiness = max(0, lateness).
