📈 Trend

A consistent long-run rise or fall across the entire series. Values systematically move in one direction — not just up and down randomly.

🔄 Seasonality

A fixed, repeating pattern tied to a calendar period (monthly, quarterly). The same peaks and troughs recur every cycle at predictable intervals.

∿ Cyclical

Broad, multi-year wave-like swings with no fixed period. Driven by economic expansions and contractions — longer and less regular than seasonal patterns.

🎲 Random

Irregular, unpredictable variation with no discernible structure. No method can reliably forecast pure random variation from past data alone.
