Product Variety and EOQ

Challenge objective: decide when expanding product variety helps versus when it increases inventory carrying cost with limited demand gain.

Core content
  • Product substitution occurs when people are willing to buy a less-preferred version if their preferred version is not available.
  • Expanding product variety is good if rate of product substitution is low, so total demand for all versions rises.
  • Expanding product variety is costly if rate of product substitution is high, so total demand for all versions rises little or decreases.
